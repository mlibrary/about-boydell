  This package is a compilation of two of the other packages on offer: Music and Performing Arts, containing 60 books. For institutions that are interested in enriching their collections on the performing arts holistically by opting for both packages, this combined package allows them to do so with a smaller administrative burden. This combined package includes 60 essential titles on the theory, history, and scholarship of music and film, theatre, and dance. It covers a broad geographical area with titles on Hispanic and Lusophone dance, German Cinema, African theatre and cinema, and Classical European music. All titles are in an EPUB as well as PDF format for enhanced accessibility.

Due to rising publication costs, a 2.5% annual increase has been applied for annual payments. The slight annual rise in membership fees reflects rising inflation rates alongside rising production costs for publishers globally. This enables us to continue to use membership revenue solely to produce new OA monographs, moving towards an affordable, sustainable model for the future of academic publishing. And while the rises are around 2.5%, in real terms they are no more than a rise of around $20 to $80 a year.
